About me

I was born and raised in Roswell, Georgia and moved to Palo Alto, California in 2019. I attended Carnegie Mellon University for a B.S. in Mechanical and Biomedical Engineering. Currently, I am completing my Masters in Biomedical Engineering at Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, Georgia.

CIA Buggy Racing Team

During undergrad, I was co-president of the CIA Buggy Racing Team. Buggy is a century-old sport and tradition at Carnegie Mellon in which independent student teams design, build, and race carbon fiber gravity racers. CIA Buggy operates with 5 main subfunctions: Mechanics & Build, Pushers, Drivers, Data, and Social. As co-president of CIA Buggy, I was continuously working with our officers to create the best experience for our members and to optimize these team functions and have a successful raceday, which led to 1st place wins in 2024 and 2025.

Ceramics, painting, and jewelry making

In my free time, I enjoy exploring creative mediums like pottery, acrylic paint, and metallurgy. These activities help me explore new ways to design, create physical forms, and work through mistakes, which are beneficial when working on real world applications.
