The Avery is an extending desk organizer designed to be opened out for creative activities and stowed away when not in use. Focusing on the user’s desire for organic shapes, The Avery is designed to be a playful addition to any space while providing discrete organization.

This project was completed for 51-264 Product Design Fundamentals.
